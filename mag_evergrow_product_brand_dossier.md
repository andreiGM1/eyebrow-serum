## 6. How to Use & Ritual Protocol

To achieve maximum density and structural length, incorporate MAG EVERGROW into your nightly skincare ritual:

1. **Purify:** Ensure all makeup, oil cleansers, and eye creams are completely removed. The eyelid margin and brow area should be clean, dry, and balanced.
2. **Dispense:** Dip the precision applicator into the elixir, wiping any excess liquid against the rim of the 3 ml vial.
3. **Target Lashes:** Gently glide the applicator tip along the root of the upper lash line (similar to applying liquid eyeliner), starting from the inner corner outwards. Avoid blinking into the eye.
4. **Target Brows:** Sweep the remaining serum directly across sparse patches, arch contours, and thinning brow sections using light, upward strokes.
5. **Repair Overnight:** Leave undisturbed overnight. The non-greasy lipid complex absorbs slowly while your body undergoes cellular rest and repair. Rinse with warm water during your morning ritual.
